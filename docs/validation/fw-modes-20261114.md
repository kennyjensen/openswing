## Equations and independent numerical checks

The [authoritative index](aswing-authoritative-references.md) and source
catalog were consulted again. Drela's **Unsteady Extension**, printed
pp. 26–29, Eqs. 130–132 and 144–149, defines the perturbation matrices as
`A = ∂R/∂x`, `M = −∂R/∂xdot`, with `A v = λ M v`, and gives the shifted
operator `(A − μM)⁻¹ M` for Arnoldi searches. The current checks use that
full descriptor formulation, retaining algebraic states and avoiding modal
reduction. Four small searches at different shifts find ten roots. A
one-to-one minimum-distance assignment matches nine to the figure; the
extra candidate remains separate.

Maximum left/right backward error against the saved matrices is
**9.40e−17**. To check beyond the eigenvalue algebra, the physical system was
reconstructed at the saved trim, with residual **2.678e−11**. Each eigenvector
was normalized to maximum component magnitude one. The real and imaginary
parts of `(v, λv)` were then applied to the **unmodified full nonlinear
residual**, using fourth-order symmetric differences at the two preselected
widths **2e−4 and 4e−4**. Numerically negligible imaginary parts of real modes
were skipped.

All **34/34** directional checks pass the predeclared 1e−7 threshold. The
largest residual, normalized by
`||A z||∞ + ||M zdot||∞`, is **3.691e−11**. This includes the unmatched real
root. The check exercises the original equations rather than multiplying
the assembled matrix again; it supports the state/rate linearization along
these modes. It does not independently establish every physical equation,
all state-space directions, or the forced-response input/output derivatives.

The PDF was visually inspected and its vector markers re-extracted. The
entire JSON reference, including all twelve operating points, reproduces
exactly after normal JSON tuple-to-array conversion. The original mesh,
executable version and some settings remain unspecified. The inferred
unpowered-glide trim and other setup limitations from the preceding study
still apply.
