Independent continuum derivation
================================
For the linearized spatial fields z=(u,theta,delta N,delta M), the exact material
constitutive law and geometrically exact kinematics imply

 u'       = theta cross r0' + R0 D^-1 R0^T (delta N - theta cross N0)
 theta'   = R0 B^-1 R0^T (delta M - theta cross M0)
 delta N' = 0
 delta M' = -u' cross N0 - r0' cross delta N.

Here D=diag(GKc,EA,GKn), B=diag(EIcc,GJ,EInn). The theta cross N0/M0 terms retain
the physical force and torque prestress. The root has u(0)=theta(0)=0, and the
six independent tip conditions prescribe delta N(L) and delta M(L).

continuum_compliance.py has no OpenSwing imports. It integrates six fundamental
solutions, initialized with the six root internal-wrench basis vectors and zero
root motion, using a 12x6 state. A 6x6 endpoint-wrench map then supplies all six
tip load solutions. DOP853 tolerances (rtol,atol)=(3e-11,3e-13) and (1e-12,1e-14)
are both retained. This is an independent continuum linear BVP; it does not reuse
production geometry, compliance, residual assembly, finite differences or modes.

The steady ASWING formulation supplies the underlying symmetric constitutive
relations (16-20), kinematics, and spatial balances (46-47):
https://web.mit.edu/drela/Public/web/aswing/asw_theory.pdf
The present incremental equations are explicitly derived from those continuum
relations, not claimed to reproduce ASWING's own discrete grid or Jacobian.

Closed-form cross-check
=======================
For tau=0, a bending plane satisfies EI*theta''=P*a*theta-a*F,
a=lambda-P/G. With zero root motion and tip moment T, let
k=sqrt(abs(P*a/EI)). For P*a>0, t=tanh(kL)/k and c=sech(kL);
for P*a<0, t=tan(kL)/k and c=sec(kL). Then

 u(L)/F     = L/G + (a/P)*(L-t)
 u(L)/T     = theta(L)/F = (1-c)/P
 theta(L)/T = t/EI.

The zero-P limits are L/G+L^3/(3EI), L^2/(2EI), L/EI. The x/theta_z plane has
negative cross-coupling signs; z/theta_x has positive signs. Axial and torsional
terms are L/EA and L/GJ. The independent numerical continuum matrices agree with
these exact expressions within 5.54001e-14 dimensionless component difference.

Dead spatial torque and rotational chart terms
==============================================
A constant spatial end torque is not the gradient of a global orientation
potential on SO(3). It must not be confused with a material follower torque or
assumed to yield a symmetric physical tangent at a twisted loaded base.

For left-chart rotation R=Exp(q)R0, the chart covector is Jl(q)^T times the
physical moment. Its first derivative adds (1/2)*skew(M0). At equilibrium the
same chart derivative of the prescribed dead tip torque cancels that term in
the full residual. Production therefore differentiates the actual physical
internal restoring moment, with the constant physical external load subtracted
implicitly. It does not pull back only the internal moment and discard the
external-load connection term.

For the continuum endpoint stiffness Ktip=C^-1, let G be the 6x6 matrix whose
rotational block is skew(M_tip), with all other blocks zero. The independently
derived identity is

 Ktip - Ktip^T + G = 0.

In the stated normalization the raw skew part is 0.64 for tau=0.8; it correctly
remains nonzero under refinement. The corrected continuum identity holds within
7.44649e-13. The extra production finite-mesh defect after adding G decreases
quadratically; its largest 32-element entry is 0.00282485 dimensionless. At zero
torque the production corrected defect is below 5.75e-11. This separates the
physical dead-torque effect from the known strong-form midpoint discretization
error, and avoids misdiagnosing raw nonsymmetry as a new defect.

Actual production measurement
=============================
production_compliance.py constructs unmodified StaticNetwork objects. At every
perturbed geometry it eliminates the mixed constitutive equations through the
actual compliance, then calls the unmodified static residual. Gravity, mass,
aerodynamics, struts, point objects, offsets and damping are absent. Interior
base wrenches vanish and the endpoint wrenches match the exact preload; constant
dead external loads have zero physical derivative. Root reactions need not be
included when the root motion is fixed and its equations are removed.

Every free nodal mechanical coordinate is centrally differentiated. Six unit
dimensionless loads at the tip are solved simultaneously. Both complete matrices
and all six nodal solutions are preserved; no eigenvalue search or static
nonlinear continuation is used. The production tangent source and all 58 kernel
module bytes are pinned before and after execution.

Limits and reproduction
=======================
These exact axial/twist bases have zero axis offsets, finite diagonal section
stiffness, and uniform geometry. They do not represent the actual Durrant wing's
coupled bending stiffness, offsets, rigid compliance directions, piecewise
properties, nonuniform bending/torsion or aerodynamic equilibrium. Agreement of
six boundary-driven response columns does not prove the complete distributed-load
operator or nonlinear dynamic energy conservation. The loaded Durrant continuum
tangent and experimental flutter agreement remain separate unfinished work.
