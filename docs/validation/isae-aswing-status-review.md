## Reference acceptance that the evidence supports

1. Declare a coverage manifest first: reference file and raw row, operating coordinate, every required output channel, normalization/axis convention, source version and allowed setting. Distinguish inputs/index columns from predicted outputs; preserve duplicate rows, missing rows and failures. Require every declared target, rather than an RMSE over surviving rows.
2. Preserve P-1's existing absolute CT gate2e−12 for the110 nominal-diameter values. It predates this review and already tests the complete stored curve scope; no retrospective widening is needed.
3. For printed scalar tables, derive resolution from each original token. With documented round-to-nearest output, the reference interval is value±half a last displayed unit. If formatting is unproved, label that assumption and retain a conservative full-unit alternative; do not call the interval experimental uncertainty. A difference of two rounded positions receives the sum of their endpoint bounds. Derivatives/angles reconstructed from printed components need propagated bounds, not an invented fixed tolerance.
4. Separate print consistency from solver/mesh verification. Exact discrete reproduction should use the archived mesh/settings if recoverable and demand the same printed result, with a prospective floating-point/iterative budget. For different meshes, use an independently established numerical error envelope. Two-grid absolute differences alone do not rigorously bound either continuum error or the unknown ASWING discretization error. A defensible provisional diagnostic is whether the reference interval intersects the coarse/fine response interval, explicitly labeled mesh compatibility; it is not an exact-reproduction pass.
5. A future approximate engineering match may use a user-agreed per-channel tolerance, declared before new comparisons and expressed in physical units or a fixed documented scale near zero. This is distinct from print-precision reproduction. Do not pick1% or a larger allowance because it happens to cover today's maxima, do not hide failing channels behind average error, and do not use experimental uncertainty for an ASWING-to-OpenSwing gate.
6. EPS curves require their own coordinate-quantization and axis-transform bounds. Where an abscissa is quantized, propagate that uncertainty through response slope or evaluate interval coverage. The retained excluded vertices remain excluded with reasons; image/display resolution must not be replaced by a broad percent gate.

Pazy illustrates why these distinctions matter: at5°/56m/s the frozen fine displacement error is0.631271mm while its printed subtraction bound is0.00055mm; the elevation error is0.00318122° versus0.000005° print bound. Its fine/coarse differences at that point are about0.000772mm and0.00015517°. Thus the close curves and passing mesh gate do not make the remaining archive discrepancy a rounding effect. The completed full sweep need not be repeated just to establish this.

Recommended immediate ledger labels are **complete stored component curves reproduced** (P-1), **complete selected-channel comparisons with unresolved discrepancies/settings** (SA-2/3/5/8 and Pazy), and **selected curve/field/frequency references compared** (ST-3). A small current-source all-output manifest/replay of SA-3 is a stronger next reference benchmark than expanding experimental sweeps. Full-polar targets should include dimensional lift/drag and efficiency as well as CL/CD/Cm/CDi, with their exact definitions checked before execution. Pazy work should concentrate on the still-unknown producing conditions or remaining output discrepancies, not another unchanged flag sweep.
