Independent derivative of the implemented objective mixed beam element
=====================================================================
This checks the actual current loaded C16 discrete compatibility operator.
It does not claim this discretization is historically identical to ASWING.

Physical material endpoint frames are Ra=Qa R0a and Rb=Qb R0b.
k=log(Ra^T Rb), Rbar=Ra exp(k/2), dr=rb+Qb offb-ra-Qa offa.
The residual is [(Rbar^T dr-v0-ds (S l)_u)/L;
                k-k0-ds (S l)_theta], l=[Rbar^T N; Rbar^T M].
ds and material compliance are fixed original material quantities. The
restored state contains full loaded N0,M0 and deformed endpoint frames.

For spatial endpoint rotations theta_a,theta_b:
 dk=Jl(k)^(-1) Ra^T(theta_b-theta_a)
 theta_bar=theta_a + Ra Jl(k/2) dk/2
 ddr=du_b-du_a+theta_b cross ob-theta_a cross oa
 dl=[Rbar^T(dN-theta_bar cross N0);
     Rbar^T(dM-theta_bar cross M0)]
 dc=[(Rbar^T(ddr-theta_bar cross dr)-ds(S dl)_u)/L;
     dk-ds(S dl)_theta].
Jl and its inverse are evaluated by closed Rodrigues expressions/series;
no production SO(3), interpolation, local_motion or tangent helper is used.
The independent raw residual uses scipy.spatial.transform.Rotation and is
tested by a fourth-order central stencil at fixed widths 2e-4 and 1e-4.

Rigid objectivity requires du=t+phi cross r, theta_a=theta_b=phi,
dN=phi cross N0, dM=phi cross M0. Holding resultants fixed while rotating
the geometry is not an objectivity mode. We retain separate N0 and M0
transport actions in all four saved harmonic direction parts.

Production imports are limited to constructing and restoring the public
tagged physical-breaks-v1 network, DynamicSystem coordinate conversion,
and sampled input material/mesh properties. Section stiffness/axis couplings
are guarded zero for this literal; independent diagonal reciprocal stiffness
is compared with production S. Constructor compliance inversions and its
Euler 3x3 coordinate solve are existing constructor work, not aircraft solves.
No production geometry/tangent helper is used to compute expected values.

References: .reference/asw_theory.txt equations16-20 (material constitutive
law and continuum kinematics),48-54 (published grid equations; different
parameterization, no historical parity claim). Native contract inspected at
openswing/network.py constitutive_frame/geometry/residual compatibility,
openswing/linear_dynamics.py DynamicSystem.unpack spatial-increment chart,
openswing/structural_outputs.py loaded local-resultant derivative. These are
source provenance, not expected numerical tangent calls.

Excluded: nodal/global force balance, aero/gravity/inertia assembly, live
brace/joint follower-load tangent, full moving-root curved-beam impedance,
historical reference trim, and the full-aircraft near1Hz Bode discrepancy.
