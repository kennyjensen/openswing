What is averaged

Each attached gyro measures aircraft-frame angular velocity plus local elastic angular velocity. The sensor z axes share the loaded aircraft’s body-z direction. We average the complex responses before calculating magnitude and phase, preserving cancellation between sensors. Each sensor has equal weight within the displayed layouts.

  • Cabin + wing root (2): pilot attachment and wing center.
  • Cabin + wing root + both wingtips (4): those two points and the left/right tips.
  • 7 aircraft locations: pilot attachment, wing root, both tips, mid-boom, stabilizer center and mid-fin.
  • All 103 nodes: one gyro at every structural node. This average depends on mesh density.

Haverage(f) = (H1(f) + … + HN(f)) / N. The downloadable locations include beam, station, loaded coordinates in feet and exact weights. Sensors add no mass or stiffness. This is a measurement diagnostic; the normal Bode output and dynamics are unchanged.
